Payment rules

Payments and package rules

Effective 3 November 2026

Buying and renewing

Packages are prepaid. The full displayed package price is due each time you buy, renew, or switch. Any payment-provider fee is shown separately in the Mobile Money approval prompt. Never share your Mobile Money PIN with NDAHI Connect.

Validity begins when payment is verified, not when you first connect. Renewal buys a new validity period for your current eligible package. There is no automatic renewal or recurring debit. Daily can be purchased again after the active bundle expires or its data is exhausted, including on the same day. Each purchase requires your approval and starts a new 24-hour validity period when payment is verified. Discontinued packages cannot be renewed.

Active bundle protection

Use your existing bundle until its validity ends or its data allowance is exhausted. You cannot buy, renew, upgrade or switch to another package while that bundle is active. Sign in to connect with your existing bundle. Once eligible, you can buy the same available package or choose another at its full price; there is no automatic renewal or prorated credit. An explicitly authorized administrator may replace a bundle for support or compensation, with confirmation and an audit record.

A payment submitted earlier does not grant permission to replace a still-active bundle. Confirmed money is retained in payment history and sent for support review if activation conflicts with existing access. A review is not a completed refund.

Interrupted or pending payments

Closing a tab or checkout does not cancel a payment already submitted. Approve only the existing phone prompt. Use “Resume saved payment” on the same browser, or sign in and recheck the payment in Payment history. A timeout does not establish that a charge failed, so do not send another payment while the approval window is open. If a payment is still not confirmed when its payment request window ends (normally five minutes, or earlier when the gateway reports expiry), it is closed and you can start a new payment. Closed payments are checked for late confirmation for 24 hours, and signed provider callbacks can still be verified later. A late confirmation activates your package when eligible; if you have already bought another active package, the extra payment is held for support review. Any refund requires review and provider confirmation. Contact support if a charge cannot be confirmed.

Cancellation and refunds

You can leave checkout before requesting payment. Once submitted, a payment must be checked with the provider; closing checkout does not reverse it. To stop future purchases, simply do not renew. Access otherwise ends when validity expires or the data allowance is exhausted.

After confirmed payment, request a refund from Payment history and explain the issue. Support reviews requests before submitting a full package refund. There is no automatic prorated refund for unused time or data. A request does not guarantee approval. These service rules do not limit any rights you have under applicable law.

Requested means awaiting support review. Pending means provider confirmation or payout is outstanding. Completed means the provider confirmed the refund. Failed means the provider reported failure and support must review it. Confirmation times depend on the provider. On a confirmed refund, any still-active access purchased with that payment is revoked; other packages are unaffected.

Receipts and help

Each confirmed payment has a downloadable receipt in Payment history and an emailed receipt when an account email and delivery service are available. Receipts record package price, allowances, validity and provider reference. They remain available after a refund. If email delivery is delayed, download your receipt from the dashboard. When contacting support, include your payment reference; never send your PIN or activation code.

Never share your Mobile Money PIN with NDAHI Connect.
