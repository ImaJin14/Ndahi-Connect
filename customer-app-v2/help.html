    Common questions

    My payment went through but I'm not online

    Open Activity and tap the waiting payment to check it. Most confirm within a minute.

    Can I use my bundle on my laptop too?

    Yes, while you have a free device slot. Your bundle has a set number of slots; when they're full, replace a device in Devices.

    Can I buy a new bundle before mine ends?

    Not yet — use it until it expires, then buy any bundle. Package rules

    Can I share my bundle through my phone's hotspot?

    Personal bundles restrict sharing: they work on your registered devices only. To share with a household through a router, choose Home WISP Unlimited.

    How do I collect a shop order?

    Bring your order code to the pickup point shown on your order. Staff check it before handing over the device.

    How do I connect to NDAHI Wi-Fi?

    Join the NDAHI Wi-Fi network, open the portal and sign in with your account number, voucher and PIN. Use Connect this device when offered. Keep the Wi-Fi connection on while the router prepares access.

    Open my portal

    How do I connect by scanning a voucher QR?

    Open your phone's camera and point it at the QR on your voucher slip, then open the link it shows. NDAHI opens that voucher on this device so you can connect. Stay on NDAHI Wi-Fi while you do this.

    Type the code instead

    I forgot my PIN or no longer have my old number.

    Use Forgot PIN for a reset link sent to your linked email, use a saved account backup code, or use an enrolled passkey to sign in. Generate account backup codes from Account while you can still sign in. Using one resets the PIN and revokes the whole set. Complete any additional verification required by your current account security policy. If you have no working sign-in method or linked email, contact support for an identity check and an owner-approved, time-limited recovery code. A payer number or voucher screenshot alone is not proof of account ownership.
