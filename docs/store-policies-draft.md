## 1. Store terms and ordering

NDAHI Store sells networking equipment and accessories. The seller is **[legal seller name]**, at **[business address]**. Contact us at **[support phone/email]** during **[support hours]**.

You must have legal capacity to enter this purchase, or act through an appropriately authorised legal representative. This does not waive protections available to minors. You may buy as a guest or sign in to your NDAHI account. Give accurate contact details so we can discuss your order and arrange pickup. Guest checkout does not create an internet account or change an existing account's details.

Prices are shown in FCFA (XAF). Review the product, quantity and final total before approving payment. Any applicable tax or additional charge must be shown before payment. We will not add an undisclosed charge after you pay.

An order reference records your checkout attempt. A pending payment is not confirmation that money has been received. A paid order awaiting review is not yet ready to collect. Please wait for the order status to show that it is ready. This status does not give us an unlimited right to delay fulfilment, keep payment or change a concluded contract.

Proposed contract process for implementation: choose a product, enter details, review the complete order and total, correct errors, then expressly confirm the purchase and approve payment. We promptly acknowledge receipt electronically and provide a separate acceptance confirmation with its date/time and a retainable copy of the agreed terms. Payment status, contract acceptance and readiness for collection must be clearly distinguished. **The final acceptance event, integrity/signature method and downloadable contract still require implementation and legal verification; this paragraph is not a claim that they are already available.**

The offer is valid for the duration stated in the listing. Price changes apply to new orders, not a concluded purchase. If no different fulfilment period was expressly agreed before purchase, our proposed commitment is to make the order available within 15 calendar days of contract conclusion. If we cannot fulfil it, we inform you in writing and refund the amount due without requiring you to accept a substitute. A stock or price error does not automatically release us from obligations already binding under law.

If a stock or listing error affects an order, we will contact you and offer an available alternative for your agreement or a refund. We will not substitute a different model without permission. No term in these policies removes rights provided by applicable law.

## 2. Payments and payment problems

Use the payment methods displayed at checkout. A Mobile Money payment requires your approval through your provider. NDAHI staff will never ask you to disclose a Mobile Money PIN, account password or one-time security code.

Your contact number and payment number may differ. Keep your order reference and provider transaction reference. If money was debited but the order remains pending, contact support before making another payment; we will check the payment status.

If payment arrives after a stock reservation expires, we will review availability before confirming fulfilment. If we cannot supply the item, we will arrange a refund. A refund is complete only when its status is confirmed; we will not describe a pending provider request as completed.

## 3. Pickup and collection

Orders are collected at **[pickup address]**, during **[pickup hours]**. Delivery is not offered at launch. Any preparation estimate is shown or communicated when we confirm the order.

Wait for the ready-for-pickup status before travelling. Bring your order reference and collection code. Keep the code private. If someone will collect for you, contact support first to agree the collection arrangement.

At collection, check the model, package contents and visible condition with our staff. Keep your receipt and any recorded serial number. Collection or a signed receipt does not waive a later valid defect or return claim.

If you cannot collect, contact us. We propose contacting buyers after 7 days and reviewing uncollected orders after 30 days. We will attempt contact and explain the next step; we will not automatically keep your payment or impose an undisclosed fee.

## 4. Cancellations, returns and refunds

Contact **[support contact]** with your order reference to request cancellation or return. No customer account is required. We will explain any ownership check and return arrangement; internal approval steps do not shorten your legal deadlines.

**Before collection:** ask us to cancel as soon as possible. If payment has been received and the order has not been collected, we propose a full refund. If payment is still being verified, we will reconcile it before confirming the refund outcome.

**Change of mind:** for ordinary networking hardware, you may notify us of withdrawal without giving a reason or paying a cancellation penalty within at least 15 working days starting the day after collection. If required pre-contract information was not supplied, the implementing decree provides an extended three-month withdrawal period. If the missing information is supplied within that period, it provides a fresh 15-working-day period from receipt of that information. We will honour the longer deadline required by the applicable texts; the final date-counting wording requires local legal review. We will not treat installing or trying ordinary hardware as an automatic waiver. We currently propose no special withdrawal exception for ordinary routers/extenders. Any future lawful exception must be disclosed before purchase and cannot remove rights for defects. Reasonable inspection does not automatically remove return rights. Return the device and supplied accessories; tell us about missing items or damage so we can explain any lawful consequence before proceeding. Retain packaging where possible, but missing packaging alone does not cancel statutory rights.

**Wrong, damaged or faulty item:** contact us promptly and describe the issue. Photos or a serial number can help, but reporting after an arbitrary short period does not automatically invalidate legal rights. We will arrange assessment and the remedy available in your circumstances, including replacement, repair or refund as appropriate under applicable law. We propose covering reasonable agreed return costs.

**Refund process:** we will explain the amount, method and expected date. We propose initiating the refund within 3 business days and completing it without fees within 15 calendar days after receiving your withdrawal notice, or sooner if law requires. Internal approval, an inspection appointment or a provider ticket does not restart or extend that deadline. We will agree the return arrangement promptly; we do not impose a blanket right to withhold reimbursement until inspection. Refunds use the same payment conditions, without a forced store credit or restocking penalty. Provider delays do not remove our responsibility to resolve the refund. Use the original payment route; any lawful alternative requires informed agreement and verified payer entitlement, with no extra cost or delay. Applicable interest or other legal remedies for a late refund are not waived. No blanket “all sales final” rule applies.

Remove personal settings and accounts before returning equipment where safe to do so. Do not erase evidence needed to diagnose a fault; ask us if unsure. We will never ask for your internet account password or Mobile Money PIN.

## 5. Warranty and technical support

Statutory remedies for defects and nonconformity are separate from a voluntary warranty and are not limited to its duration. Each product listing will state its actual warranty, provider, duration and how to request service. Manufacturer cover varies by model. We propose an additional 30-day store guarantee for manufacturing faults only after supplier support and cost are confirmed.

Explain the problem and provide your order reference. We may inspect the device to distinguish a fault from setup or upstream network problems. Damage caused by misuse, liquid, unsuitable power, or unauthorised modification may fall outside voluntary cover where it caused the fault; this does not remove statutory rights or cover for an unrelated defect.

We remain responsible for obligations owed by the seller; we will not require you to deal only with a manufacturer or payment provider. Any used, refurbished or repaired condition must be clearly disclosed before purchase and on the receipt. Equipment must meet applicable safety and regulatory requirements, and supplied instructions must explain safe use and warranty arrangements.

We will explain any paid service and obtain your agreement before charging. Opening a support case does not guarantee an immediate replacement, but we will give a clear next step and updates.

## 6. Compatibility and internet access

An extender needs a usable upstream connection. A travel router's captive-portal and operating-mode support depends on the exact model, firmware and network configuration. We identify required power, cables and operating modes in the listing.

We use “Tested with NDAHI Connect” only for the model/configuration actually physically verified. Buying hardware does not itself include an internet bundle, increase device allowances, remove network rules or guarantee a particular speed or coverage. Any included setup or internet service must be expressly stated in the offer.

If you are unsure about compatibility, contact support before ordering. An inaccurate compatibility claim in our listing is handled under the returns policy; this paragraph does not excuse a misleading description.

### Corrections from the first draft

- Withdrawal: decree art. 13 says **15 working days**, not the first draft's proposed 15 calendar days. It also provides an extension where information was not supplied; art. 14 addresses later disclosure and trying goods. The policy preserves the longer applicable deadline.
- Refund trigger: decree art. 20 starts the fifteen-day clock on receipt of withdrawal notice; the earlier law art. 20 refers to return of goods. The draft adopts the earlier notice trigger rather than delaying until approval/inspection. Counsel must reconcile the authoritative texts and counting rules.
- Return costs: law art. 20(4) and decree art. 16 distinguish ordinary withdrawal and wrong goods. Offering seller-funded agreed return costs is an optional customer benefit, not a claim that every statutory withdrawal requires the seller to pay.
- Preservation: decree arts. 8 and 22 specify ten-year electronic evidence obligations. A blanket “delete all data on request” promise would conflict with required preservation; retaining all technical logs for ten years is not justified by this alone.
- Consumer law arts. 6 and 13 require French/English contracts and information. An English-only draft cannot be treated as ready to publish.
- A short voluntary warranty cannot replace statutory rights, seller after-sales duties or remedies for hidden defects. Consumer law art. 5 restricts exclusions and unilateral arbitration clauses.
- Data law ss. 19 and 32 address prior processing and international-transfer authorisation; s. 22 addresses breach notification. A privacy page or a provider contract alone does not prove those duties are satisfied.
