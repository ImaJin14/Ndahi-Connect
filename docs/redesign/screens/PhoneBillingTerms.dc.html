Payment rules

Payments and package rules

In force from 3 November 2026

Buying and renewing
  • Bundles are prepaid. You pay the full price each time; any provider fee shows in the Mobile Money prompt.
  • Validity starts when the payment is confirmed, not when you connect.
  • No automatic renewal or recurring debit, ever.
While a bundle is active
  • Use it until it expires or its data runs out. You can't buy or switch packages before then.
  • After that, buy any package at its full price — there's no partial credit.
Interrupted or pending payments
  • Closing the page doesn't cancel a payment you already sent. Approve the prompt that's on your phone.
  • Don't pay again while a request is open (usually five minutes).
  • Late confirmations are checked for 24 hours.
Cancellation and refunds
  • Any refund needs a review and confirmation from the payment provider.
Receipts and help
  • Every confirmed payment has a receipt in Activity.

NDAHI will never ask for your Mobile Money PIN.
