# Designs added on 13 Nov 2026

Everything designed on 13 November, with what each design does and how we'll judge whether it worked. Screen sources are in [`screens/`](screens/). Product rules are in [`decisions.md`](decisions.md).

## How to read the success rating

Each design gets a **success rating from 1 to 5**. It's a pre-launch estimate of how likely the design is to make a clear, measurable difference. It combines:

- **Impact:** how many customers or how much money it touches.
- **Confidence:** how sure we are the design solves the problem without new risk.

| Rating | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| ★★★★★ | Fixes a known pain on a main path; very likely to show up in the numbers |
| ★★★★☆ | Clear benefit on a common path; small unknowns |
| ★★★☆☆ | Useful, but depends on how often it's used or on a policy choice |
| ★★☆☆☆ | Nice to have, or the benefit is hard to measure |

Each design also has a **success measure**: the metric and a proposed target to check after launch.

- The targets are starting points for the owner to adjust, not commitments.
- Measure on the new design against the current design using the `ui` variant tag from Phase 0. Where the current design has no equivalent, measure against the month before launch.
- Re-rate each design after 30 days at 100%.

## Details

### 1. Get-connected flow board — ★★★★★

**Screens:** `ConnectFlow` (new board); `PhoneHotspot` and `PhoneVoucher` (updated).

One board shows every path from joining NDAHI Wi-Fi to being online, built from the real screens:

1. I have an account.
2. I have a code: a voucher, or a gift reserved for a number.
3. Scan a QR.
4. I'm new, buy.
5. Use someone's bundle.

A sixth row shows the recoveries: not on Wi-Fi, someone else signed in, forgot PIN, payment declined, network down. The hotspot option is now "I have a code" and mentions scanning. The voucher screen gains "Scan the QR instead" and a gift-claim link. Its old "top up" copy is replaced, because top-ups break the billing rule.

**Why this rating:** this is the path every paying customer takes, so small fixes show up directly in revenue.
**Success measure:** share of Wi-Fi page visitors who end up online, new vs current design. Target +5 points.

### 2. Payment didn't go through — ★★★★★

**Screens:** `PhonePaymentFailed` (new; variants: declined, low balance, timed out, wrong network); `PhoneBuyBundle` (adds a "Payment didn't go through?" link).

Each failure explains what happened, states whether money was taken, shows a reference, and gives one next step. The timed-out state tells people not to pay again and keeps checking, so nobody is charged twice.

**Why this rating:** Mobile Money failures are common, and today they end in confusion or double payments.
**Success measure:** duplicate charges per month (target 0); share of failed payments followed by a successful one within 15 minutes (target ≥ 40%).

### 3. Renewal moments — ★★★★☆

**Screen:** `PhoneRenew` (variants: 80% used, ends tomorrow, ended).

A push notification and home-screen state at 80% data used and the day before the bundle ends. Once it ends, a one-tap "Buy [PLAN] again" pre-fills the last plan and Mobile Money number. The screen respects the rule that customers can't buy while a bundle is active and links to the explanation.

**Why this rating:** the end of a bundle is the moment customers drift away, and this removes steps right there. The rating is below 5 because the effect depends on notification permission.
**Success measure:** share of customers who buy again within 2 days of a bundle ending. Target +10 points.

### 4. Give a voucher to an existing customer — ★★★★☆

**Screens:** `AdminVouchers` (new "For one customer" mode next to "Batch"); `AdminCustomerDetail` ("Give a voucher" action).

The admin searches by name or number, picks a bundle, and marks it Sold or Gift. It starts immediately. If the customer already has an active bundle, the admin must tick a confirmation before it's replaced. This matches the current admin's behaviour.

**Why this rating:** a daily staff task with a real risk of mistakes, now with a guard.
**Success measure:** median time to give a voucher (target < 30 s); accidental replacements reported (target 0).

### 5. Vouchers for unregistered numbers — ★★★★☆

**Screens:**
- `AdminVouchers`: reserve a voucher for a number with no account.
- `PhoneClaimGift` (new): number + code → create PIN → start now or later. Variants: from NDAHI or from a friend, and code for the wrong number.
- `AdminVoucherDetail`: new "waiting for claim" state with change-number, days left and wrong-attempt count.
- `PhoneLogin`: "Someone gave you a bundle? Claim it" link.

There's no SMS. Claiming needs the number **and** the code. Paying with Mobile Money from that number also claims it. Unclaimed reservations expire after 90 days.

**Why this rating:** it opens gifting and walk-in sales to people with no account. The rating isn't 5 because it relies on the code getting from the giver to the person.
**Success measure:** share claimed within 7 days (target ≥ 70%); claims by the wrong person (target 0).

### 6. Buy for someone else — ★★★☆☆

**Screen:** `PhoneBuyForOther` (new; variants: recipient has no account, has an account, has an active bundle).

A customer pays with their own Mobile Money for another number. What happens next depends on the recipient:

- **No account:** they get a code to share on WhatsApp.
- **Has an account:** it goes straight into their account.
- **Bundle active:** it's saved until their current bundle ends.

**Why this rating:** it fits shared community Wi-Fi, but adoption is uncertain.
**Success measure:** share of bundle sales bought for someone else. Target ≥ 5% within 3 months.

### 7. Customer filters: active and stale — ★★★★☆

**Screen:** `AdminCustomers`.

The customer list gets these additions:

- Filter chips: **All · Active · Stale · Suspended**, each with a count.
- A "Stale after" setting: 7, 14, 30, 60 or 90 days.
- A "Last active" column and a "Stale · N days" badge.
- A "Give voucher" link on stale rows, and a CSV export of the stale list.

**Why this rating:** it turns a passive list into a win-back tool.
**Success measure:** stale customers who buy again each month, tracked from launch. Set a target once there's a baseline.

### 8. Bulk gift voucher or message — ★★★☆☆

**Screen:** `AdminCustomers` (updated).

Tick rows, or select every stale customer, then give them all a gift voucher or send a message. The message goes as an in-app notification plus email.

**Why this rating:** it can bring people back, but the outcome depends on the offer, not the screen.
**Success measure:** share of gifted stale customers who buy within 30 days. Target ≥ 15%.

### 9. Voucher detail page — ★★★★☆

**Screen:** `AdminVoucherDetail` (new; states: in use, not used, used up, waiting for claim, revoked).

- A ticket-style card with the QR code, print and copy.
- Usage: data, time and devices.
- A history timeline.
- Details: customer, type, batch, router sync, receipt email.
- Actions matching the current admin: mark sold/gift, resend email, revoke with an on-page confirmation.

**Why this rating:** "what happened to this code?" is the most common voucher question, and now one page answers it.
**Success measure:** staff can answer voucher questions without opening the audit log. Check this in the Phase 5 week of operations.

### 10. Outages — ★★★★☆

**Screens:** `PhoneOutage` (new; down and restored); `AdminOutage` (new; detected, live, fixed); `AdminSide` (adds Outages).

The admin posts a notice in English and French. It shows on the Wi-Fi page and in the portal, and customers with an active bundle can be notified. The admin posts updates, then marks the outage fixed and chooses how much time to give back.

**Why this rating:** it protects trust when customers' time keeps running during downtime. The rating isn't 5 because the compensation policy is still open (see below).
**Success measure:** support contacts during an outage, compared with past outages. Target −50%.

### 11. French layout check — ★★★☆☆

**Screens:** `PhoneHomeFR` (new); `TabBar` (now takes `lang="fr"`).

The home screen and tab bar in French, to catch labels that run about 25% longer than in English.

**Why this rating:** a prevention step. It's valuable but has no direct metric.
**Success measure:** no clipped or wrapped labels in French at 360 px on the main screens.

### 12. Reseller home — ★★★☆☆

**Screen:** `ResellerHome` (new).

The reseller's own phone view:
- Show a code and QR to a customer, then tap "Cash received".
- See codes in hand, sales this week, commission, and what they owe NDAHI.
- Ask for more codes, and print their codes.

**Why this rating:** it reduces disputes, but depends on resellers using it.
**Success measure:** cash disputes with resellers (target −50%); remittances on time (target ≥ 90%).

### 13. Insights and design funnel — ★★★★☆

**Screens:** `AdminInsights` (new); `AdminSide` (adds Insights).

- Revenue per day.
- Busiest hours.
- Access-point load at peak, with a capacity warning.
- **Wi-Fi page → paid funnel, new vs current design.**

**Why this rating:** the funnel is what makes the rollout decisions safe.
**Success measure:** the owner checks it before each `UI_V2_PERCENT` step, and capacity problems are spotted before complaints.

### 14. Speed limit per bundle — ★★★☆☆

**Screen:** `AdminBundleEdit` (updated).

Download and upload Mbps per customer, applied as a MikroTik queue. The preview shows "up to N Mbps".

**Why this rating:** it helps fairness at peak, but the right limits need tuning.
**Success measure:** "slow in the evening" complaints. Target −30%.

### 15. Usability test script — ★★★★☆

**File:** [`usability-test.md`](usability-test.md).

Five real customers, eight tasks, and a pass bar to meet before the new design goes past 10% of visitors.

**Why this rating:** it's cheap and catches problems before they reach everyone.
**Success measure:** run before ramping; tasks 1, 2 and 4 pass for at least 4 of 5 people.
