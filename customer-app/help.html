Frequently asked questions

How do I connect to NDAHI Wi-Fi?

Join the NDAHI Wi-Fi network, open the portal and sign in with your account number, voucher and PIN. Use Connect this device when offered. Keep the Wi-Fi connection on while the router prepares access.

Open my portal

The login button does not work on my phone or iPad.

Wait for the page to finish loading. If the Wi-Fi sign-in popup stops responding, stay on NDAHI Wi-Fi and open the portal in your full Safari, Chrome or Samsung Internet browser. Use PIN sign-in if passkeys are unavailable. Do not share your PIN or voucher with anyone.

Try sign-in

Can I check my balance away from the network?

Yes. You can sign in, check balances and history, download vouchers and manage your profile from another internet connection. Buying, renewing and switching packages require NDAHI Wi-Fi.

Check my balance

Can I buy when my data has run out?

Yes. Join NDAHI Wi-Fi; you do not need an active bundle or passing internet to request payment. If your phone asks, choose Use without internet. If the portal still asks you to connect, temporarily turn off mobile data and select I’ve connected — check again. Your checkout details stay on the page. Contact support if the message remains with mobile data off.

My payment is pending, expired, or money was deducted without a bundle.

Follow the countdown and approve the existing Mobile Money prompt once. There is no extra checkout-blocking grace period. A confirmed gateway expiry releases checkout immediately; otherwise the displayed request deadline applies. When the server closes an unconfirmed checkout, its record stays in history. Expiry does not prove that no money moved. If money was deducted, recheck the original payment or contact support before paying again. A later confirmed payment is activated once or held for review if it conflicts with another purchase. Never approve an old prompt after starting a replacement payment.

Can another number pay for my renewal?

Yes. Enter that number in the renewal payment form and choose its Mobile Money network. It is used only for that transaction. The next new checkout defaults to your account number; resuming an existing checkout keeps its original payer. Your profile and login number do not change.

Where can I find and download my voucher codes?

Open Vouchers to copy a code or download its PDF. Current and historical codes remain available after expiry. Downloading an expired, replaced or refunded voucher does not reactivate it. Keep downloaded codes private.

Open vouchers

My device limit is full. How do I disconnect a device?

Use Home → Connected devices. A device slot is released after the router confirms disconnection. A pending or failed operation may still occupy the slot. Devices that leave Wi-Fi are removed after the network reachability grace period and a successful device-list update. Closing the portal alone does not disconnect a reachable device.

Manage devices

What happens when I renew or switch packages?

A confirmed renewal or switch starts a new validity period. You can renew or switch only after your existing bundle expires or its data is used up. While it is active, sign in to connect with that bundle; another customer purchase cannot replace it. Review the price, data allowance and device limit before approving payment. There is no automatic renewal.

How do refunds work?

Request a refund from a confirmed payment in Payments and explain the problem. Support reviews the request. Requested, submitted and completed refunds are different states; a delay or timeout does not prove that a refund has been paid. Keep your payment reference and contact support when a refund needs attention.

Open payments

I forgot my PIN or no longer have my old number.

Use Forgot PIN for a reset link sent to your linked email, use a saved account backup code, or use an enrolled passkey to sign in. Generate account backup codes from Account while you can still sign in. Using one resets the PIN and revokes the whole set. Complete any additional verification required by your current account security policy. If you have no working sign-in method or linked email, contact support for an identity check and an owner-approved, time-limited recovery code. A payer number or voucher screenshot alone is not proof of account ownership.

Open PIN recovery

Can I change my account number or email?

Open Account. Number changes require fresh verification with your current PIN or a passkey; follow any additional verification requested during a security transition. No SMS is sent. Check the number carefully: it becomes your login and default payment number. Email remains optional; confirm a linked email to receive account messages and email PIN recovery.

Open account

Will the installed app update automatically?

Open the portal while online to receive the current version. New releases can refresh an idle portal; payments, verification and unsaved forms delay refresh. You do not need to reinstall or link an email for app feature updates. Email notifications and device usage alerts are separate from app updates.
